Click the roof to read the temperature. About the readings
Apparent surface temperatures at emissivity 0.95, captured 14:20–14:38 in cloudy conditions with a low November sun. The camera sees the surface of the gravel, not the membrane beneath: wet areas read as cold patches in otherwise even gravel. Shaded areas beside upstands and plant also read cold. Not reliable on metal or glass (flashings, ducts, rooflights, glazing). Accuracy approx. ±2 °C, so best used to compare nearby spots. The south-east part of the roof comes from a second processing of the same flight images and agrees with the rest of the map to within about 0.3 °C. The dark angular shapes in that area are shadows from the neighbouring structures, not moisture.
